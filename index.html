Descriptive Statistics

Kruskal-Wallis Test

H₀: all graders draw from the same score distribution. Non-parametric rank-based test; appropriate when graders are randomly assigned and scores are ordinal. η² is the effect size (0 = no effect).

Dunn's Post-Hoc Test — Bonferroni-corrected pairwise p-values

Shown because the overall Kruskal-Wallis test was significant. Highlighted cells (p < 0.05) indicate a grader pair with a statistically significant difference in score distributions.
